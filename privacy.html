Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ClipPilot handles information when you use the ClipPilot desktop application (“ClipPilot” or the “App”). ClipPilot is a local desktop tool for Twitch stream monitoring, clip creation and review, vertical video editing, and optional publishing to connected platforms.

Important: ClipPilot is designed to run its application backend and store its working library on your own computer. When you connect third-party accounts or publish content, information is exchanged directly with the relevant platform services. Your use of those services is also governed by their own privacy policies.

1. Information ClipPilot may handle

  • Account connection information: authorization tokens and account identifiers needed to connect to Twitch, YouTube, or TikTok. These are used to access the platform features you enable.
  • Stream and chat information: Twitch channel information, stream details, chat messages, and related activity that the App uses to identify potential moments.
  • Media and clip information: recordings, extracted clips, edited vertical videos, thumbnails, titles, descriptions, hashtags, review decisions, scores, and other metadata created or managed in the App.
  • App settings and records: agent configurations, publishing preferences, queue status, upload results, and local library/database records.
  • Support information: information you choose to provide when reporting a problem or contacting the project maintainers.

2. Where information is processed and stored

The App is designed to run its backend locally on your computer and to keep its working database, settings, queue, and media files in local application storage. The App may use local tools and models installed on your computer for tasks such as clip analysis or title suggestions.

When you connect accounts, retrieve platform data, or publish a video, the App communicates with the relevant third-party services using their official APIs or authorization flows. Information required for those actions is sent to the platform concerned. ClipPilot does not need a ClipPilot-hosted account service to perform its core local workflow.

The exact information retained on your computer depends on how you use the App and your settings. Anyone with access to your Windows account or computer may potentially be able to access locally stored App data.

3. How information is used

Information handled by ClipPilot is used to operate the features you choose, including monitoring configured Twitch channels, detecting and reviewing moments, creating clips and vertical videos, managing your library and publishing queue, connecting platform accounts, and uploading content at your direction or under settings you enable.

4. Third-party services

ClipPilot can interact with Twitch, YouTube/Google, and TikTok. When you authorize a connection or use an integration, those services may receive information and process it under their own terms and privacy policies. ClipPilot does not control their practices, availability, API rules, or retention periods.

  • Twitch Privacy Notice
  • Google Privacy Policy
  • TikTok Privacy Policy

5. Publishing and your content

If you enable publishing, ClipPilot may send the video file and associated metadata, such as title, description, and hashtags, to the YouTube or TikTok account you authorize. Review your content and account selections before enabling automatic uploads. Published content is then subject to the platform’s settings, moderation, and retention practices.

6. Retention and deletion

Local clips, recordings, settings, database entries, and connection data may remain on your computer until you delete them or remove the relevant local App data. You can manage or delete media through the App where those controls are available. You can revoke ClipPilot’s access through the connected platform’s account-security or authorized-app settings. Revoking access prevents future authorized API actions but does not necessarily delete content already uploaded to that platform.

To remove all locally stored ClipPilot information, close the App and use the App’s available deletion/uninstall procedures and your operating system’s file-management tools to remove its local data. Take care: this may permanently delete clips, settings, and publishing history.

7. Security

ClipPilot uses local application storage and platform authorization mechanisms. No software or storage method is completely secure. Keep your computer and user account protected, do not share your authorization credentials, and revoke access if you believe an account connection has been compromised. Do not assume locally stored credentials are encrypted unless the specific build you use documents that protection.

8. Children

ClipPilot is not intended to be used in a way that violates the minimum age requirements or terms of Twitch, YouTube, TikTok, or applicable law. Do not use the App to collect or process another person’s information unlawfully.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when ClipPilot’s features or data practices change. The updated version will be posted at this page with a revised effective date. Check this page periodically.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests relating to ClipPilot, contact the project maintainers through the ClipPilot GitHub repository. Do not post passwords, access tokens, or other secrets in a public issue.
